LOCAL STATEMENT COLLECTOR
Venmo statement downloader
Personal and business monthly CSVs, from 2019 onward. Saved directly to your chosen project folder. No payments or Wave transactions are changed.
Ready for setup.
1. Connect and choose the destination
Choose your wave-solver/data/venmo folder once. Chrome keeps this folder access local.
2. Capture statement sources
In Venmo Statements, choose All profiles and a completed month with Download CSV buttons. Open Venmo defaults to the previous month; the current month may not have statements yet. Confirm that the first transaction Download CSV belongs to your personal profile and the second belongs to your business profile, then Capture both sources. The Sales tax collected CSV is skipped. You can also capture each profile separately. Capture triggers Download CSV to read the real download URL rather than guessing an endpoint. The sample download stays in Chrome's Downloads folder; the collector saves its own copies to the project folder.
Statement controls / troubleshooting
3. Download and resume
Both profiles are collected one month at a time, newest completed month first, working back through 2019. The current partial month is attempted last. Months returning HTTP 204/404/410 are recorded as unavailable and skipped. All profiles can stay selected throughout the run. If a separate profile switch cannot be identified, the run pauses: switch profiles in Venmo, then Resume. Existing verified files are skipped; changed CSVs receive a new filename.
