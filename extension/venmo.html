LOCAL STATEMENT COLLECTOR

Venmo statement downloader

Personal and business monthly CSVs, from September 2019 onward. Saved directly to your chosen project folder. No payments or Wave transactions are changed.

Ready for setup.

1. Connect and choose the destination

Choose your wave-solver/data/venmo folder once. Chrome keeps this folder access local.

2. Link the All profiles statement page

Open All profiles in Venmo on a month with both transaction Download CSV buttons. Confirm personal is first and business second, then Use All profiles page. The collector opens each month once, downloads both files, and moves to the next month. Sales-tax CSV is skipped; no separate profile switching or sample-source capture is needed.

Separate-profile fallback

Statement controls / troubleshooting
3. Download and resume
Both profiles are collected one month at a time, newest completed month first, working back to your start month (September 2019 by default). Earlier months are not requested. The current partial month is attempted last. Months without download buttons after waiting are recorded as unconfirmed and skipped; login and file errors stop the run. All profiles stays selected throughout the run. Each month downloads personal and business before moving on. Existing verified files are skipped; changed CSVs receive a new filename.
