LOCAL STATEMENT COLLECTOR

Venmo statement downloader

Personal and business monthly CSVs, from 2019 onward. Saved directly to your chosen project folder. No payments or Wave transactions are changed.

1. Connect and choose the destination

Choose your wave-solver/data/venmo folder once. Chrome keeps this folder access local.

2. Capture statement sources

In Venmo Statements, choose All profiles and a month. Confirm that the first transaction Download CSV belongs to your personal profile and the second belongs to your business profile, then Capture both sources. The Sales tax collected CSV is skipped. You can also capture each profile separately. Capture triggers Download CSV to read the real download URL rather than guessing an endpoint. The sample download stays in Chrome's Downloads folder; the collector saves its own copies to the project folder.

Statement controls / troubleshooting
3. Download and resume
Both profiles are collected one month at a time. All profiles can stay selected throughout the run. If a separate profile switch cannot be identified, the run pauses: switch profiles in Venmo, then Resume. Existing verified files are skipped; changed CSVs receive a new filename.
Ready for setup.
