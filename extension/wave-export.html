Business and mailbox settings

The mailbox number is the /u/ number in Gmail’s address. The helper verifies the signed-in mailbox, reads only the matching Wave export message, and fetches its fresh ZIP directly into data/wave. You do not need to open the Downloads folder. The email search refreshes every five seconds for up to ten minutes; Stop and Resume preserve the request.
